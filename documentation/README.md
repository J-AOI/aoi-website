# AO Integrity Documentation

This directory contains the permanent internal documentation supporting the AO Integrity research program.

Contents:

- **PUBLICATION_LOG.md** - Chronological record of every public publication.
- **CHANGELOG.md** - High-level release history for the research program and website.
- **MILESTONES.md** - Significant milestones in the development of AO Integrity.
- *(Future)* **RESEARCH_BIBLIOGRAPHY.md** - Master index of published research.
- *(Future)* **DECISIONS.md** - Architectural and editorial decisions affecting the research program.

These documents are intended to preserve institutional continuity as the AO Integrity body of research grows.
