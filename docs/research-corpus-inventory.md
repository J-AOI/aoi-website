### Research Library Model

- Every public item should have a stable ID, slug, status, date, format, abstract, related concepts, related frameworks, and downloadable asset references.
- Concept pages should be first-class reference pages. Briefs and frameworks should attach to concepts such as Authority Drift, Staleness Window, Runtime Admissibility, Decision Receipts, Governance Evidence, and Memory Admissibility.
- Framework pages should be durable canonical definitions. RRBs, Governance Questions, Field Notes, and Narrative Briefs should cite frameworks rather than restating definitions from scratch.
- Research Briefs should become the main serial publication lane for compact, numbered technical/research artifacts. Governance Questions should be a separate numbered series because they function as inquiry prompts and conceptual tests.
- Narrative Briefs should be kept separate from Research Briefs because they are explanatory and audience-facing, not formal research records.
- Working Papers should house long-form thesis material and major arguments before they are decomposed into shorter briefs and framework pages.

### Relationship System

- `Framework -> Concepts -> Briefs`: each framework owns definitions and links to supporting RRBs, Governance Questions, and evidence notes.
- `Working Paper -> Extracted Publications`: long-form thesis documents should link to the RRBs and narrative briefs derived from them.
- `Governance Question -> Related Evidence`: each GQ should link to accumulated evidence, field notes, and relevant framework definitions.
- `Platform -> Framework Mapping`: ValidateAuthority.ai pages should map capabilities to framework concepts: changed authority, execution state, staleness window, evidence package, and decision receipt.
